Our Process

Quality, Care and Professional Export Coordination

A structured approach from buyer inquiry to shipment coordination, with clear alignment on requirements.

01 — SOURCE

Product Sourcing

Sourcing is approached through appropriate agricultural and supplier networks based on the buyer's product requirements.

02 — SELECT

Product Selection

Product selection is discussed and aligned according to agreed buyer expectations, product characteristics and availability.

03 — CHECK

Quality Checks

Products can be evaluated according to applicable buyer specifications and agreed quality requirements. Any specific inspection or testing requirement should be agreed before order confirmation.

04 — PACK

Packaging

Packaging can be coordinated according to product requirements, buyer preferences, shipment conditions and destination needs.

05 — DOCUMENT

Documentation

Export documentation can be coordinated according to applicable shipment, buyer and destination requirements.

06 — SHIP

Shipment Coordination

We support professional coordination from order confirmation through shipment preparation and agreed dispatch arrangements.
